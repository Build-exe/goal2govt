# 🎯 Goal2Govt

**Goal2Govt – Your Goal. Your Government Job.**

Goal2Govt is a platform designed to help students and job seekers discover the latest **government job notifications, exam updates, results, admit cards, syllabi, and preparation resources** in one place.

🌐 **Website:** goal2govt.com

---

## 🚀 About Goal2Govt

Finding the right government job notification shouldn't be difficult.

Goal2Govt aims to provide simple, organized, and easy-to-understand information about government recruitment and competitive examinations across India.

Our goal is to help aspirants stay updated and move closer to their dream government job.
